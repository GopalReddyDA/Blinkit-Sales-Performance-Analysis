# Blinkit Sales & Performance Analysis

## Project Objective :
To analyze Blinkit's sales and outlet data using Excel and SQL and identify key business insights from sales, customer ratings, product categories, outlet types, sizes, and locations.

The objective is to use facts, evidence, and data to understand business performance and support better decision making.

## Project Overview :
This project analyzes Blinkit retail data to understand:
* Sales performance
* Customer ratings
* Item category performance
* Fat content preferences
* Outlet size and location performance
* Outlet establishment trends

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) :
* **Total Sales:** Total revenue generated.
* **Average Sales:** Average revenue per sale.
* **Number of Items:** Total number of items sold.
* **Average Rating:** Average customer rating.

## Business Analysis :
### 1. Total Sales by Fat Content
Analyze sales of **Low Fat** and **Regular** products.
**Purpose:** Understand customer preferences and product demand.

### 2. Total Sales by Item Type : 
Compare sales across different item categories.
**Purpose:** Identify high-performing and low-performing categories.

### 3. Fat Content by Outlet
Compare Low Fat and Regular product sales across outlets.
**Purpose:** Understand product preferences by outlet.

### 4. Total Sales by Outlet Establishment
Analyze sales based on the outlet's establishment year.
**Purpose:** Understand sales trends across new and older outlets.

### 5. Sales by Outlet Size
Analyze sales across different outlet sizes.
**Purpose:** Understand whether outlet size affects revenue.

### 6. Sales by Outlet Location
Compare sales across different outlet locations.
**Purpose:** Identify high-performing locations.

### 7. All Metrics by Outlet Type
Compare all KPIs across different outlet types.

**Metrics:**
* Total Sales
* Average Sales
* Number of Items
* Average Rating
**Purpose:** Compare outlet performance and identify key business trends.

## Tools Used
